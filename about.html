About Smart Digital Seva

Welcome to Smart Digital Seva!

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to help visitors find useful information about certificates, identity services, government schemes, banking information, education, employment and document tools.

  • Banking and recharge information
  • J&K land records and revenue links

Independent Platform

Smart Digital Seva is not an official government website, government department or authorised CSC portal. Official applications and approvals are handled by the relevant authorities.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide clear information and convenient access to useful links. Visitors should always verify details on official websites before applying for any service.
