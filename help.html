5. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is Smart Digital Seva an official government website?

Ans. No. It is an independent information platform.

Q. Are the photo and PDF tools free?

Ans. Yes, the tools provided on this website are intended to be free.

Q. Can I apply for a government certificate here?

Ans. Our website provides information and links. Applications must be completed on the appropriate official portal.

Q. Why is an external website not opening?

Ans. The official website may be temporarily unavailable or its address may have changed.

Q. Does this website store my documents?

Ans. The browser-based photo and PDF tools process files locally on your device. Do not upload sensitive documents to unknown websites.
